# João Duarte Soares Gonçalves

Supply Chain Consultant at Hilti Group (Sourcing Excellence), applying AI to procurement and supply chain work.
M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering and Management, FEUP (Porto), 2026. Thesis on combining LLMs with deterministic rule checks to validate supply chain documents.
Interested in production AI: LLM agents, guardrails, evaluation pipelines and retrieval (RAG).

## Projects

- [Vehicle model recognition](https://github.com/joaosoaresgoncalves/vehicle-model-recognition-cnn): CNN that identifies Mercedes-Benz and smart models from photos, plus K-means colour detection. Built during an internship at a Mercedes-Benz dealer, 2023.
- [GAN augmentation for carotid ultrasound](https://github.com/joaosoaresgoncalves/gan-ultrasound-augmentation): GAN-generated images added to the training data of four CNN classifiers; AlexNet validation accuracy went from 0.924 to 0.942. Paper with Ricardo Fitas, JEECOM 6(1), 2024, [doi:10.33650/jeecom.v6i1.8006](https://doi.org/10.33650/jeecom.v6i1.8006).
